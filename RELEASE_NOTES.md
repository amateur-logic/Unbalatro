# UnBalatro 2.0.0

The first full release of **UnBalatro**, a mod for the [Balatro3DS](https://github.com/idkhan/Balatro3DS) fan port. It adds **15 Jokers** and **10 Decks**, all with their own artwork.

## Install

1. Download `UnBalatro_2.0.0.zip` below.
2. Unzip and copy the `UnBalatro` folder to `/3ds/save/Balatro3DS/sdmc/mods/` on your SD card.
3. Launch the game and check **Options > Mods** for UnBalatro.

## What's in it

**Jokers:** Aces Up, Triggered Joker, Wizard Joker, Bizarro Joker, Perkeo Jr., Infinity Joker, Freedom Joker, Sisyphus, Hyperspace, Mario, Luigi, Link, Samus Aran, Kirby, Donkey Kong.

**Decks:** Conjurer, Vagabond, Stacked, Adventurer, Faceoff, Patio, Collector, Hei, Elevated, Jockler.

Full effects, costs and rarities are in the [README](README.md).

## Good to know

- Saved runs are tied to the mod version. Finish or abandon a run before updating.
- Kirby can only copy Blueprint-compatible Jokers. Blueprint and Brainstorm can copy Wizard, Freedom, Samus Aran and Perkeo Jr.; the other UnBalatro Jokers are deliberately not copyable.
- Donkey Kong is the only Legendary Joker in the mod. Kirby is Rare.
- This mod is meant to be unbalanced. Link starts at X15 Mult and gains X5 for every Boss Blind, so expect very big numbers.

## Found a bug?

Please open an [issue](../../issues) with your Jokers and Deck, what happened, and `mods/mods_log.txt` if the game showed an error.
